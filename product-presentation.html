The problem

Quarterly checks create a blind window.

A borrower can deteriorate between reporting cycles. Payments, cash flows, facility use and industry conditions may all be flashing warning signs — while the covenant file still reads “compliant.”

By the time a formal breach is discovered, intervention options have narrowed. The issue is not a lack of data; it is disconnected, unread evidence.
