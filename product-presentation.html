The problem

Quarterly checks create a blind window.

A borrower may start facing financial trouble between reporting periods. Payments, cash flow, credit use, or industry conditions can show early warning signs, even when the latest covenant report still says “compliant.”

By the time a formal breach is discovered, intervention options have narrowed. The issue is not a lack of data; it is disconnected, unread evidence.
